Explore the High Country

A quick note before you set out. Hours, road access, and trail conditions change — the Blue Ridge Parkway has periodic closures, and some areas are still recovering from Helene. Confirm before you go.

Grandfather Mountain — Cross the Mile High Swinging Bridge, take in expansive mountain views, and explore wildlife habitats and nature exhibits. One of the area’s most recognizable attractions.Best for: first-time visitors, families, photographers.

Blue Ridge Parkway & Linn Cove Viaduct — A scenic drive with overlooks and mountain landscapes, including the famous viaduct wrapping around Grandfather Mountain. Check current road closures rather than assuming the full route is open.Best for: scenic drives, photography, fall foliage.

The Blowing Rock — The namesake cliff attraction, with mountain views and short walks through the grounds. Pairs well with time in downtown Blowing Rock.Best for: couples, families, scenery without a long hike.

Moses H. Cone Memorial Park — A historic estate with lake scenery and an extensive carriage-trail network. A good alternative to a rugged mountain hike.Best for: leisurely walks, history, a relaxed outdoor afternoon.

Julian Price Memorial Park & Price Lake — Lakeside scenery, walking, and picnic spots along the Parkway.Best for: families, nature lovers, quieter outings.

Rough Ridge — A popular mountain-view hike near the Parkway, often counted among the area’s standout hikes.Best for: active visitors and photographers.

Linville Falls — One of the region’s signature waterfalls, with different hiking routes and viewpoints.Best for: waterfall enthusiasts, hikers, photography.

Otter Falls — A waterfall outing in Seven Devils, convenient to the Banner Elk area.Best for: a waterfall-focused excursion.

Elk River Falls — An impressive 50-foot waterfall near Elk Park; the approach includes steep steps. Treat it as a waterfall-viewing outing rather than a swimming spot.Best for: waterfall photography, a shorter excursion.

Elk Knob State Park — A hiking destination near Boone, beyond the Parkway corridor.Best for: visitors whose priority is hiking.

Boone Greenway — A convenient outdoor walk without committing to a mountain trail.Best for: families and gentler outings.

Rocky Knob Mountain Bike Park — A dedicated mountain-biking destination near Boone.Best for: mountain bikers and active travelers.

Guided Whitewater Rafting — An organized adventure; area outfitters offer trips suited to a range of visitors.Best for: adventure-seeking families and groups.

New River Paddling — A river outing around New River State Park — a different side of the High Country.Best for: paddling and a water-based day trip.

Tweetsie Railroad — A classic Wild West-themed attraction and one of the region’s best-known family destinations.Check the operating calendar and special-event schedule.

Wilderness Run Alpine Coaster — A mountain coaster near Banner Elk — a memorable thrill without taking up the whole day.Check reservation requirements and rider restrictions.

Wilderness Run Adventure Course — An adventure course that pairs well with the coaster visit.Confirm operating dates and participation requirements.

Apple Hill Farm — Guided visits to meet alpacas, llamas, and other farm animals — a distinctive stop beyond the usual sightseeing.Plan around scheduled tours.

Sugar Creek Gem Mine — A hands-on gem-mining stop in the Banner Elk area.A good fit for a family-oriented itinerary.

Mystery Hill — A longstanding attraction along the Boone–Blowing Rock corridor.Check the available experiences before choosing tickets.

Wildcat Lake — A small lake near Banner Elk with paddling and fishing opportunities.Confirm current access, amenities, and rental availability.

Tate-Evans Park — A convenient town-park stop alongside a visit to downtown Banner Elk.Note: the park has undergone post-Helene rebuilding — check current conditions.

Sugar Mountain Resort — A principal skiing and snowboarding destination, convenient to Banner Elk.

Beech Mountain Resort — A signature winter-sports destination; the mountain also offers warm-season hiking and mountain biking.

Appalachian Ski Mtn. — A winter-sports option in the Boone–Blowing Rock area.

Hawksnest — Snow tubing and zipline experiences — mountain adventure beyond skiing.Check which activities are offered during your visit.

Downtown Banner Elk — Combine a meal with a stroll through town; the dining scene is a notable part of Banner Elk’s appeal.

Downtown Boone & King Street — Shopping, restaurants, and local breweries — a useful change of pace from outdoor sightseeing.

Downtown Blowing Rock — Shops, galleries, and restaurants in a scenic High Country day trip.

Valle Crucis & the Original Mast General Store — A classic stop for small-town character and a historic general-store experience.

Grandfather Vineyard & Winery — A wine-tasting option in the Banner Elk–Boone area.

Banner Elk Winery — Close to Banner Elk, and the first winery established in the Banner Elk–Boone area.

Boone Farmers Market — A seasonal way to experience local food and community rather than only the major attractions.

Boone Golf Club — A public golf option to round out the activity list for a day on the course.

Banner House Museum — Local history and 19th-century life in Banner Elk.Tour season is limited — confirm dates before planning around it.

Hickory Ridge Living History Museum & Horn in the West — Regional history and outdoor theater.Check their respective schedules.

Woolly Worm Festival — A distinctive Banner Elk fall tradition built on mountain folklore and woolly bear caterpillars.

Choose-and-Cut Christmas Tree Farms — A memorable seasonal outing for late-fall and holiday visitors.

Fall Foliage Drives — A signature seasonal experience combining mountain scenery with stops in nearby towns.

  1. Banner Elk family outing — Wilderness Run Alpine Coaster, Sugar Creek Gem Mine, and a meal downtown.
  2. Scenic mountain day — Grandfather Mountain, then accessible portions of the Parkway and Price Lake. Check road conditions when choosing the route.
  3. Boone & Blowing Rock day — King Street shopping and lunch, then downtown Blowing Rock and either Moses Cone Memorial Park or The Blowing Rock.
  4. Relaxed countryside outing — Valle Crucis, the original Mast General Store, and a scheduled tour at Apple Hill Farm.
  5. Active outdoor day — Pick one main hike or waterfall — Rough Ridge, Linville Falls, or Otter Falls — rather than trying to fit in several.
